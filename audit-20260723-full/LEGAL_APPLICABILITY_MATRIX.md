## Gate B2B — is CloneStore actually B2B-exclusive?

**Finding: no.** `src/app/signup/page.tsx` (evidence file 01/02) collects only fullName/companyName(optional)/email/password via direct Supabase Auth — no professional-status confirmation, no company-name requirement, no VAT field, no "j'agis en tant que professionnel" checkbox, no CGV/CGU acceptance at all before this block's fix (which added only a passive link, not a professional-status gate). A private individual can create an account and reach checkout today.

This does **not** mean CloneStore must reject sole traders without a VAT number (the master prompt explicitly warns against that) — but it does mean CloneStore currently reads, technically, as **potentially B2C**, which pulls in the full consumer-rights/mediation/DSA-adjacent analysis rows above rather than allowing them to be marked N/A. **This is the single most consequential finding in this matrix**: every "conditional" row above resolves cleanly to "not applicable" the moment a genuine B2B gate (company name required + professional-capacity confirmation, per the master prompt's own suggested checklist) is implemented and verified — until then, treat FR/BE/LU/CH consumer-law exposure as live, not theoretical.

## What this matrix does NOT do
It does not certify that any of the "Yes, applicable" rows have actually been satisfied — only that the law applies. Satisfaction status lives in the dedicated matrices (`PRIVACY_DATA_PROCESSING_MATRIX.md`, `DPA_COMPLIANCE_MATRIX.md`, `COOKIE_AND_TRACKER_INVENTORY.md`, `AI_ACT_AND_HR_RISK_MATRIX.md`, `TAX_AND_PRICE_DISCLOSURE_MATRIX.md`).
