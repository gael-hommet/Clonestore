Demo and Mobile Conversion Closure, 2026-07-24

Problem: GuidedTourProvider already auto-shows a floating welcome card on "/" after a
flat 1200ms timer (PUBLIC_DISCOVERY_WELCOME). The new DemoContextualPrompt auto-shows a
second floating card on "/" after 35% scroll depth. Without coordination, both could be
visible on screen at once once the new prompt is enabled — forbidden by this block's gate.

Fix: src/components/guided-tour/GuidedTourProvider.tsx's homepage auto-welcome effect now
calls shouldSuppressHomepageAutoWelcome(pathname, isDemoContextualPromptEnabled()) from
src/lib/demo/contextual-prompt/detect.ts, short-circuiting to setWelcome(null) whenever
pathname === "/" AND the new flag reads "true". Manual tour launch, other routes' tours,
and all snooze/progress storage are untouched (the guard runs before any of that code).
